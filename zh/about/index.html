ABOUT AQUAMARINE

Beauty and wellness, thoughtfully brought together.

Aquamarine is a hair and beauty destination on East Coast Road, bringing thoughtful care for hair, scalp and skin together in one welcoming space.

Care begins with the right choices.

We believe a better beauty experience begins with choosing the right products, technologies and treatments for each individual.

Rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach, Aquamarine brings together professional expertise, considered techniques and trusted professional brands to create care that feels personal, purposeful and comfortable.

A little time, just for you.

From a haircut or colour to a restorative Head Spa or personalised facial, we want every visit to feel considered from beginning to end.

Our aim is simple: to create a welcoming place where professional care, trusted products and thoughtful service come together — so you can leave feeling looked after, refreshed and confidently yourself.
